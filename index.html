filmography

As Irmsko remakes borrowed film scenes with friends, failed takes and an unfinished voice message gradually become a film of their own.

built from a camcorder tape documenting a year of family celebrations in 2000, year of gold layers present-day reflections on belonging. the archive became both an obsession and a space where memory and identity intersect. birthdays and eid gatherings blur into wedding-like rituals through a recurring family joke woven across the footage.
